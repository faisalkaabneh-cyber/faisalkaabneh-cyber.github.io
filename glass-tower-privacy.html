Privacy Policy

Glass Tower: Gem Smash

Developer: Faisal AlkaabnehEffective: 24 November 2026Platforms: iOS and Android

Glass Tower is a free puzzle game where you shatter colored glass to free a gem. This policy explains what happens with your information when you play. The short version: the developer does not collect your personal data. On iPhone, the game can show an optional video ad from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show that ad, as described below.

Data itemCollected
Name, email, phone, or account detailsNIL
Approximate location (from IP address, by Google AdMob, iOS only)ADS
Device and advertising identifiers (by Google AdMob, iOS only, IDFA only if you allow tracking)ADS
Ad interactions, performance and crash data (by Google AdMob, iOS only)ADS
Purchases or payment informationNIL

Game progress stays on your device

Your levels, stars, scores, and settings (sound, music, and haptics) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to the developer or to any server. Deleting the app deletes this saved progress.

Replay recordings

If you choose to record a level replay, your phone asks for your permission first. The video is created on your device and is shared only where you choose in the share sheet. The developer never receives it.

Optional video ads (iPhone)

  • The game shows a short, skippable ad between levels (every two finished levels), unless you buy "Remove Ads". You can also choose to watch optional videos for an extra move, a hint, a shuffle or double gems.
  • These videos are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show and measure them, Google's SDK may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, information about the ads you see and interact with, and performance and crash data.
  • The first time you open the game, iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", Google does not receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and shows non-personalized ads.
  • If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.
  • Google's use of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

What the developer does not do

  • The developer does not collect, store or sell your personal information.
  • There is no account or sign-in. Gems, skins and the optional "Remove Ads" purchase are stored only on your device; purchases are processed by Apple and the developer never sees your payment details.
  • The game can be played offline; ads simply don't load without internet.
  • The Android version currently shows no ads.

Children

Glass Tower is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future update changes how the game handles information, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
